# Become a sponsor

**Thank you for considering sponsoring BSides Amsterdam!**

BSides Amsterdam is the Netherlands’ flagship community-run information security conference.
That means vendor-neutral, practitioner-led, and built by volunteers who believe knowledge should be as free as possible.

In 2025 we welcomed 312 participants from 10+ countries, with 104 CFP submissions and 16
selected talks. Every seat was filled, and so was the waitlist with close to 200 people still wanting to get in on the action.

For 2026, we are learning from last year’s experiences and feedback and growing: More open space, more talks and a dedicated CTF/workshop area. We can only do this with partners who share our values. Your sponsorship funds the kind of event where genuine conversations happen and careers change.

# Build the Agent. Breach the Plant.
#### *An AI CTF challenge in a critical infrastructure environment*

Industrial networks were never designed for an adversary that thinks, adapts and acts at machine speed. In this two-hour Capture the Flag, you get to build one.

Design and deploy AI agents to work through a purpose-built, sandboxed OT environments. Navigate industrial systems and engineering workstations, clear increasingly difficult stages, and capture every flag you can before the clock runs out. This is not a standard CTF: it tests both your agent engineering and your understanding of how real cyber-physical systems are built, connected and protected.

Every agent is built from scratch on site. Every competitor gets the same token allocation, so efficiency matters as much as ingenuity. Prizes for top performers and swag for all participants.

Only 15 places are available, and all applications are reviewed. Participants must hold a valid BSides Amsterdam ticket. Applications close 30 October 2026.

### Pentester VS AI

[Escape](https://escape.tech/) is bringing its Pentester vs AI CTF to BSides Amsterdam with four exclusive challenges to try.

Race against the machine. AI has already captured the flag. Can you beat its time?
Test your pentesting skills, with prizes for those with the most points (completing the hardest challenges and the fastest solves) at the top of the leaderboard.

There's a challenge for everyone of any ability to get stuck into; and you might spot a little Dutch theme running through them.

Are humans or AI the better pentesters? Let's stop talking about it and put it to the real test.
